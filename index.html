From Humble Beginnings to Ice Cream Excellence:
The Story of Naturals Ice Cream

In the bustling streets of Juhu, Mumbai, 1984, Sri R S Kamath pioneered a revolutionary ice cream format. Disheartened by synthetic additives, he created ice creams from pure cow milk, pharmaceutical-grade sugar, and hand-selected fresh fruits. The concept was simple yet incredibly challenging: capture the seasonal soul of fruits like Alphonso Mango, Sitaphal, and Tender Coconut without flavorings or artificial stabilizers.

What began in a small 400 sq. ft. kitchen has blossomed into a beloved national icon. Every scoop of Naturals remains true to Kamath's vision of purity, offering a rich, creamy fruit burst that cannot be duplicated. Welcome to the legacy of natural taste.

About Us

Naturals Ice Cream has been pioneer in natural ice creams since 1984. We pride ourselves on creating handcrafted ice creams made with only three ingredients: Fruit, Milk, and Sugar.
